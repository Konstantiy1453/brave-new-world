  reports `Windows` there). **PCK: 11 cases, 220 of 220 checks. EXE: 8 cases, 175 of 175 checks**

  `docs/v17/decisions/README.md`; the records in `docs/v17/decisions/pck/` and `docs/v17/decisions/exe/`.

## What still fails or was not run

- **`v19_payback`: 11 of 42 checks miss** (1.10.1: 3; 13 of 43 before the reserve fix). Year 6: light
  mines 29–64 days against 70–150, heavy and automated 56–91 against 100–220 (crystal ones pass), the
  tailings plant 111 and sea copper 104 against 120–300; year 4: the smelter 454 against 120–300
  (1.10.1: 461) and foodworks 843 against 100–220 (1.10.1: 614). The test takes the best forecast across a bot colony. **The cause of the fast mines is not
  isolated**: visible deposit grades on the sites towns now get average 1.10–1.11 against 1.02–1.05
  on the generator's own sites (three seeds), too little to explain paybacks two to three times
  faster; the bots' colonies differ in other ways too (sites chosen by the card, supply orders).
  Not retuned.
- **Bots reach stage three later than in 1.10.1**: 3 of 6 seeds by day 720 against 5 of 6 (pacing
  records); see the harness below.
- **Suites failing as on 1.10.1 in this container**: `v17_saves`, `v17_tutorial`, `v18_systems`,
  `world_v16_seed` (fixtures and world hashes recorded on Windows), the seven `review_*` tools (their
  `D:` paths), `v16_interaction_probe` and `v16_scroll_probe` (fail the same way on 1.10.1 under Xvfb).
- **Not run**: the EXE on a Windows PC with a real GPU (frame times, the Windows-only suites in
  `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-08-v19-windows-verification-instructions.md`); human playtest.

## Repeat on Windows

From the unpacked preview folder, with the repository's `tools\verify` next to it:

```
ColonyCharter.exe --script <abs>\decisions_driver.gd -- --case=opening --coast=south --out=<file>
ColonyCharter.exe --script <abs>\decisions_driver.gd -- --case=tutorial --out=<file>
ColonyCharter.exe --script <abs>\placement_driver.gd -- --case=capital --view=planet --seed=3 --target=0 --out=<file>
```

Cases: `opening` (`--coast=south|north`), `tutorial`, `events`, `research`; placement: `capital`,
`regression`, `refusal`, `outpost` (`--bots=<folder>` with the bots copied and their `extends` paths
made absolute, since the package has no tests).

## What players can use in this build, and what still fails

**Usable, and passing its acceptance checks here:**

- **Free placement**, for the capital and every later town:
  - clicking ground on either map, the ground report, and refusals with reasons;
  - the founding button acting only on the picked point;
  - the point carried through naming, construction, expeditions, saving and loading;
  - the seed 3 regression and both capital games;
  - inland and coastal expeditions from both maps, after the normal unlock.

  The unlock itself was reached by the balance bot through `Sim.act`, not by clicks.
- **Planetary deposits per resource:** veins, pockets and barren ground, with one shared reserve
  across towns. **Prospecting one resource at a time:** orbit, field survey, drilling.
- **The radius slider** (0.70–1.30, km, gravity), with distances, travel days, route costs and orbital
  reach following it.
- **First ore truck and first ship** from recorded consignments; the notice, vehicles and location
  agree, including from a second town.
- **Mine-road cancellation:** the branch mine takes the main road.
- **Crews** use freight's road rule and wait out closures.
- **The people page:**
  - the measured 30-day change with its parts;
  - expected arrivals;
  - both unemployment thresholds;
  - the newcomers' bus to the homes it fills.
- **Forecasts and lessons:**
  - the farm forecast counts catch, other farms, queued food buildings, stock to refill and the
    delivered price;
  - commissions are quoted while blocked, with when charging starts;
  - the water lesson's three outcomes.
- **Stage three** by one operating route of three, with 600 people as an achievement, measured on six
  seeds.
- **The tutorial** opens on a bay with the mine-or-harbour decision, with inland wording.
- **The first lived-in home** shows a light on the globe and the flat map.
- **1.10.0 saves** carry over.

**Not done, or failing its acceptance test:**

- **Windows EXE:** never run. All placement checks used the shipped PCK on Linux. Until it is run on
  Windows, free placement is verified on the packaged game data but not on the Windows executable.
- **Offshore:** no offshore surveying technology, no seabed extraction, and no offshore deposit
  producing a recorded cargo. The 1.10.0 offshore signal and its survey are unchanged.
- **Nickel and cobalt:** not added.
- **Districts:** the buildable area is not larger; natural slopes are not kept until construction
  grades them; there are no rock exposures, drainage or landform clues and no new close-range
  surfaces; towns do not expand along terrain features or a real shoreline.
  - The release audit's figures (82% of mineral plots on the outer ring, 99% of starting plots flatter
    than their surroundings) still hold for the recommended sites.
  - Sites founded on picked ground take their deposit plots in a seeded order, not the outer ring
    first.
- **People:** no farmer occupation, and no single population-unit convention across recruitment,
  research teams, beds, wages and totals.
- **Crews:** no dispatch to roads, repairs or field surveys; no new standing orders; crew expansion is
  no costlier than before.
- **Events:** no local consequences by kind of town, and no later local developments.
- **The three-stage bay image:** not in the repository or this session, so the views were not compared
  against it.
- **Ship light:** at the berth it saturates into a white dot about 8 px across on the globe and the flat
  map.

District art credits (Colony Charter 1.12)

The settlement district's rocks, buildings, vehicles, plants, soil and road surfaces come from the
approved terrain and settlement handoff (docs/handoff/approved-terrain-region/region, revisions 06
and 07).

Scanned rocks (rocks/)

Poly Haven distributes the following four models and their surface maps under the CC0 1.0
Universal dedication. The game uses derived geometry (Boulder 01's field and chip meshes are
reduced from its 56,540-triangle scan), colour-graded albedo, tangent-space normal maps and packed
ambient-occlusion, roughness and metalness maps.

CC0 dedication: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Colony Charter artwork

The building and vehicle meshes (art/) come from the project's own high-poly art reserve; five of
them are cropped to their footprints with the original dimensions and pivots. The tree, shrub,
grass and outcrop meshes, the leaf atlas, the soil and turf tiles and the procedural masks (env/,
soil/) were made for the project's previews. They stay under the project's own artwork terms.

Terrain data

The world's relief, optical texture and cloud cover credit the U.S. Geological Survey, Mapzen and
NASA: see assets/terrain_v3/CREDITS.txt.
